A LITTLE CLARITY

Your questions, answered.

Is this an insurance premium calculator?

No. It divides your remaining savings goal by the months available. Insurance quotations depend on the insurer’s approved terms and your circumstances.

Can I change my target?

Yes. Change your target, savings or timeline and calculate again. If the monthly target is too high, discuss your budget and timeline with an adviser.

What happens after I share my plan?

Moses receives your details only when you press Send in WhatsApp. He can then respond to your planning or quotation enquiry.

How are my contact details used?

This website does not upload or save form details to a database. Your details stay in this page until you choose to share them through WhatsApp. WhatsApp processes messages under its own privacy terms. Moses uses your message to respond to this enquiry. You can ask him in the same chat to stop contacting you or delete your enquiry. Your contact details are not displayed publicly.
